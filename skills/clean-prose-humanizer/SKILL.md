# Clean Prose & Humanizer

## 1. Banned AI Clichés & Patterns
Eliminate stock LLM tells without changing the factual meaning:
- **Overused buzzwords**: *delve, tapestry, testament, landscape, paradigm, game-changer, revolutionize, seamless, elevate, unleash, foster, nuanced, robust, pivotal, underscore*.
- **Formulaic structures**:
  - Avoid *"It's not just X — it's Y"* / *"Not X, but Y"* contrasts.
  - Avoid forced rule-of-three lists in every paragraph.
  - Avoid dramatic one-line mic-drop closers.
  - Avoid excessive em-dashes (`—`) and repetitive bold lead-in bullet labels (`**Speed:** ... **Scale:** ...`).
  - Avoid throat-clearing openers (*"In today's fast-paced world..."*, *"Let's dive in..."*).

## 2. Natural Writing Principles
- **Lead with the point**: state the concrete fact, action, or outcome in the first sentence.
- **Vary sentence length**: mix short direct sentences with medium explanatory ones.
- **Match the medium**:
  - *README / Docs*: crisp, scannable, example-first.
  - *UX Microcopy*: short, action-oriented, zero fluff.
  - *Articles / Outreach*: conversational, specific, grounded in real details.
